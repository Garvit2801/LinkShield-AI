How one check flows, and what it runs on

A second opinion for anything forwarded to you: is it a scam, is it true, is it safe to open. Tap any box to see what it does and how much of it is built.

  • Gemini explains. It never decides.Verdicts for links and files come from fixed rules, so they are repeatable and a scam page cannot argue its way out.
  • Nothing is ever run.The sandbox opens pages and reads files on a separate machine with no cloud credentials. A clean result means no known danger signs, not guaranteed safe.
  • Without the sandbox, it says so.The link branch falls back to rules and Web Risk, and files are reported as not checked instead of low risk.
